# 🎮 Pulse Engine — Members Guide

Welcome! Here's everything you can do here. You earn **PULSE** (the server currency) **automatically** just by being active: messages, reactions and voice minutes. Spend them on games and the shop!

---

## 💰 Your profile & currency
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/balance` | Check your PULSE balance |
| `/profile` | Your profile: points, rank, PULSE (add a member to see theirs) |
| `/leaderboard` | The top players ranking |
| `/daily` | Your daily reward (come back each day for a streak bonus 🔥) |
| `/shop` | Browse the shop |
| `/buy <item>` | Buy an item |
| `/missions` | See the active missions |

---

## 🎮 Solo games (bet your PULSE)
| Command | The game |
|---|---|
| `/higherlower <bet>` | Higher or Lower — cash out before you miss |
| `/crash <bet>` | Cash out before the multiplier crashes 💥 |
| `/slots <bet>` | Slot machine 🎰 |
| `/roulette <bet>` | Roulette |
| `/blackjack <bet>` | Blackjack vs the dealer |
| `/wheel <bet>` | Gacha wheel — big multipliers possible |
| `/quiz` | Solo quiz to earn PULSE |

## ⚔️ Player-vs-player games
| Command | The game |
|---|---|
| `/duel <player> <bet>` | Challenge someone to a duel |
| `/rps <player> <bet>` | Rock Paper Scissors |
| `/typingrace` | Typing race (multiple players) |
| `/battleroyale` | Battle Royale — last one standing takes the pot |
| `/diceroyale` | Dice Royale — highest roll wins |

> **For group games** (`/battleroyale`, `/diceroyale`): click **🙋 Join**. The person who started the game clicks **▶️ Start** when everyone's ready (minimum 2 players). No time or player limit.

---

## 🎰 Lottery
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/lottery status` | See the jackpot, your tickets and the draw time |
| `/lottery buy <tickets>` | Buy tickets |

> The more tickets you hold, the better your odds. The draw is automatic 🍀

---

## 🧠 Community quizzes
Every now and then, a **quiz** starts on its own in the server. Answer with the **buttons**: you earn PULSE per correct answer, and the bonus question can be worth **double**! 🌟

---

## 🎯 Missions
Challenges pop up regularly to earn PULSE:
- ⚡ **Flash challenge** — be among the first to tap **Claim**!
- 🧩 **Riddle** — the first to find the right answer wins.
- 📅 **Daily / 🗓️ Weekly objective** — hit a goal (send messages, play games, win quizzes…) and the PULSE is credited **automatically**.

---

## 🤖 Pulsar
**Pulsar** is our host. It asks questions, launches challenges and keeps the vibe going. **Mention it or reply to one of its messages** to chat with it! 💬

---

Have fun, stay active, and climb the ranks! 🚀
